### **Editing & Production Tips for Recording**

* **Cursor Smoothness:** Use screen recording tools like *Screen Studio* or *Rotato* to add smooth 3D tilt, springy zoom-ins, and automatic cursor smoothing over your React components.
* **Sound Design is 70% of the Effect:** Do not rely only on background music. Layer real sports sound effects (ball drops, tennis/pickleball hits) right as UI elements animate or switch tabs.
* **Highlight the Framer Motion:** Emphasize your active tab spring animations, layout transitions, and card hovers with deliberate micro-zooms.
